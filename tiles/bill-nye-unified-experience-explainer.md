## ⭐ Bill Nye Says…

> “Kids!  
> 
> Imagine going to a movie theater where everyone else watches a story on a giant screen, while you sit in the corner with a robot whispering, *‘Actor is three feet to the left… now two feet to the left…’*! That wouldn't feel like a story at all—it would feel like doing math homework in the dark!  
> 
> Now imagine turning off the movie screen completely and stepping into a darkened theater—just like the **RNIB and Roundhouse** storytelling shows—where **everyone** hears the rushing river, feels the breeze, and listens to the storyteller's voice together! When **action, sound, and meaning happen as one single thing**, you don't have to translate coordinates—you just *experience* the story!”

## 🕯️ Part 1: Three Big Ideas Behind This Sanctuary

1. **No Coordinate Homework (`nondual-kernel.js`):**  
   Instead of steering a tiny dot across an invisible `(x, y)` grid while a screen reader reads percentages, every button press is a direct, meaningful gesture—like **Clapping Two Hands**, **Letting Go of the Raft**, or **Pouring Out a Cup**.
2. **A Shared Acoustic Room (`roundhouse-soundscape.js`):**  
   Softly filtered river currents, mountain wind, silk banner flutters, and bamboo water pours (`<= 780 Hz` low-pass filter) wrap around the storyteller's voice so blind and sighted listeners share the exact same calm space.
3. **Comparing Both Worlds Side by Side (`Experience Parallel`):**  
   Every stage includes a built-in **Experience Parallel** note explaining why bolted-on screen-reader telemetry splits your attention—and how designing for **unity** brings calm back into interactive art!
