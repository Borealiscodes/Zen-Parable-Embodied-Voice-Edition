## 🕯️ Why the RNIB Roundhouse Parallel Matters

In earlier prototypes like [`Zen-Parable-Audio-Sanctuary`](https://github.com), adding `aria-live` coordinate readouts to a 2D canvas severed perception from meaning. Drawing from **RNIB and Roundhouse sensory-darkness performances**, this project unites audio, physical action, and narrative into a single primary medium:
1. **Shared Acoustic-First Medium:** Sound, spatial resonance, breath, and voice *are* the room.
2. **Co-Presence of Voice and Environment:** The narrator emerges from the ambient soundscape naturally rather than via system telemetry.
3. **Reflective Parallel Inspector:** Compares *Dualist Coordinate Telemetry* directly against *Non-Dual Embodied Gestures*.

## 🍵 Embodied Non-Dual Parable Matrix

Each parable transitions from dualist mechanics to direct, unified contemplative gestures:

| Stage & Koan | Dualist Coordinate Struggle (Retired) | Unified Non-Dual Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. *The Sound of One Hand***<br>*(Master Hakuin)* | Steer `(x, y)` east with `aria-live` distance readouts. | **Clap Two Hands** (`A`) vs. **Listen to One Hand** (`D` / Shakuhachi & Tōrō chime). |
| **2. *The Raft & The Shore***<br>*(Majjhima Nikāya 22)* | Walk to piers and press state keys. | **Board the Raft** (`A`), **Step Onto Shore** (`W`), and **Let Go of the Raft** (`R`). |
| **3. *Two Monks & The Flag***<br>*(Mumonkan Case 29)* | Walk to center stone and press `Space` repeatedly. | **Argue: Wind** (`A`), **Argue: Flag** (`D`), or **Rest in Stillness** (`Space` / Taiko heartbeat). |
| **4. *The Empty Cup***<br>*(Master Nan-in)* | Walk to basin and press `R` four times. | **Add Another Opinion** (`A` / overflowing dissonance) vs. **Pour Out a Measure** (`R` / descending bamboo ladle to Bonshō bell). |
